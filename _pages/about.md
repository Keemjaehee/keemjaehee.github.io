I am a Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Information, Yonsei University. My research lies at the intersection of Information Systems, Design Science, and AI—building theory-guided, AI-based artifacts that address real-world social problems such as voice phishing. I combine social science theory with computational methods to design decision-support systems that are both effective and interpretable.

## Research Interests

- Social Science (Information Systems, Behavioral Theory)
- Design Science
- Agentic AI
- Decision Support Systems
- Voice Phishing & Fraud Detection
- Interpretable / Explainable AI
- Generative AI & LLM Applications

- **Programming:** Python, R, SQL
- **ML / AI:** PyTorch, scikit-learn, Hugging Face Transformers
- **Methods:** Machine Learning, NLP, Text Mining, Econometric Analysis
